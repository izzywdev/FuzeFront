Rules the implementation must keep

  • At ≤ 40em each grant is a card: name, email (an LTR island in RTL), granted-by/when, a full-width role select and a ≥ 44 px Remove button with an accessible name that includes the person.
  • The 409 last-owner refusal lands on that card — select snapped back to list-owner, the reason linked with aria-describedby and announced (role="alert"). Never a toast that scrolls away; never pre-emptively hidden controls (the server decides who the last owner is).
  • The role matrix becomes one row per role listing what it can do — no sideways-scrolling table.
  • Add person is a BottomSheet: org-scoped search, already-granted members shown but not selectable, roles as a radio list with their plain-language descriptions, and the confirm label names the person and role.
  • RTL: role names are localized with the role id kept as an LTR island; names and emails are bidi-isolated.
