Direct memberships only. This list shows this org's direct active memberships — one row per organization_memberships row. Principals whose access comes from above (a parent org, the platform root, or a reseller portal) are ReBAC-derived and have no membership row here, so they do not appear — by omission, satisfying the "access from above is hidden" requirement. Platform staff (Employee) are never listed here; their inherited access is surfaced only in the separate, labelled staff console (design/frames/employee-console). For the root/portal org — where "members = everyone" — this small-team list is replaced by the paginated, searchable member directory (design/frames/member-directory).

Contract. Rows come from GET /v1/security/tenants/{tenantId}/members (MemberPage — cursor-paginated; "Load more" advances page.cursor). Each member carries exactly one role from the OrgRole enum, rendered by RolePill; an unrecognised value renders a neutral pill with its raw key, never a blank cell. The self-row is tagged You. Invitations are not here — the Invite a member action routes to the org-invitations feature (design/frames/org-invitations-v2, the invite modal at this same route), so this console never re-implements invite/add.

Who sees the actions. Viewing the member list requires only membership; changing a role or removing a member requires the Permit permission organization:manage-members (held by owner/admin). The per-row actions render only for a caller who holds it — a member/viewer gets the read-only view in frame (d), and a caller who lost the permission mid-session gets the in-place 403 in frame (e). The rendered affordance is never the guard: the Security service checks Permit on every write.
