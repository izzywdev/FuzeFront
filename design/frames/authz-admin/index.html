Access & Roles · Approval Frames

The per-organization admin console at /settings/access: an owner or admin sees who has access to the organization and what role each member holds, and can change a member's role or remove their access. Authorization is Permit-backed — the UI never decides who may do what; it reflects decisions the Security service makes against Permit and writes back into Permit. Membership is one row per organization_memberships. Invitations are a separate feature (design/frames/org-invitations-v2): this console links to it, it does not duplicate it. These frames freeze the flow, its states, and the build inventory before any UI is written. Walk each frame below.

Frame (a) · /settings/access

Members & roles

The list: people with direct access, their role pill (owner / admin / member / viewer), per-row change-role / remove, and "Invite" linking out to org-invitations. Cursor "Load more".
Frame (b) · /settings/access

Change role

Ordinary promote/demote, plus the two refusals: the org's last admin cannot be demoted, and the last owner cannot be demoted.
Frame (c) · /settings/access

Remove member

Confirm removal, plus the two refusals: the org's last admin and the last owner cannot be removed.
Frame (d) · /settings/access

Read-only (non-admin)

A member/viewer can see who's in the org, but holds no manage permission — the list renders with every mutating affordance absent, not merely disabled.
Frame (e) · /settings/access

States

Loading, single-member org, load error+retry, an unauthorized action denied in place (403), the non-member state, and the root-is-member reconciliation — never a sign-in redirect.
