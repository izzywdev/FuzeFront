Contract. The user list is portal-scoped: members come from the real @fuzefront/security-client — GET /v1/security/tenants/{tenantId}/members (the tenant is this portal's org; the tenantId is resolved from the session, never accepted from the client). Roles come from GET .../roles; change-role is PUT .../members/{userId}/roles. Invite is the anticipated portal-scoped invitation route (FF-EPIC-11-S3 — not native to the monolith today): POST /api/v1/portal/invitations (InvitationCreate → 201), scoped to the caller's own portal. Inviting into a portal you don't own is refused 403 (states frame, g). You cannot change your own role (self-lockout guard).
