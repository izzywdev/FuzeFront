You don't administer that target

Installations can only be added to organizations/portals you manage. The target is resolved and authorized server-side (Permit) — a target outside your authority is refused 403, never silently added.

Contract (anticipated). (g1) in-flight GET …/installations. (g2) empty is a real state — an app installed nowhere. (g3) non-2xx with retry. (g4) read-only is fail-closed: no Add/Remove rendered. (g5) is the edge the epic calls out: an installation whose target portal was removed/suspended renders data-installation-status="orphaned" from its own record and stays removable — it must not crash or blank the list. (g6) remove is DELETE …/installations/{id} behind a confirm. (g7) add authorizes the target via Permit; a target outside the caller's authority is 403 FORBIDDEN_TARGET, in place.
