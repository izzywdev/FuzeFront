Only targets you administer are listed; a target outside your authority is not selectable.

Contract (anticipated — commissioned by these frames). App installations / scopes are not in the app-registry contract today. These frames commission a scoping surface — GET /api/v1/app-registry/apps/{slug}/installations, POST …/installations { scopeLevel, targetId, targetType }, and DELETE …/installations/{id} — modelled on the portal precedent's portal_apps. Every reference carries its type (targetType: organization / portal / user), never a bare id (identifier-standard). Orphaned target: an installation whose target portal was removed/suspended renders a data-installation-status="orphaned" pill and stays removable — it never throws. Add is scoped to targets the caller administers; a cross-tenant target is 403 (frame g).
