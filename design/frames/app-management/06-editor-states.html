Detail & editor states

The states the detail and settings editor must render: a caller with read-only authority, a suspended app with limited actions, a built-in app whose core is non-editable, an app with no editable settings, the lifecycle confirmations, and the save-time failures (validation, conflict, denial). Each is a frame QA writes a RED spec against.

Contract. (f1) read-only is fail-closed: no management affordance rendered, never a 403 on click. (f2) a suspended app offers Activate, not Suspend, and pauses installation targeting. (f3) builtin: true disables Delete client-side and the API refuses it. (f4) an app with no editable settings shows a disabled Save, not an empty broken form. (f5) suspend is POST /apps/{slug}/suspend behind a confirm. (f6) 422 renders inline, 409 preserves the form, 403 is shown in place.
