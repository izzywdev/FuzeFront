App-list states

Every state the list must render is contract, not decoration: the in-flight load, a genuinely empty registry, a load failure with retry, a no-results search, and the fail-closed case — a caller without Permit authority sees a read-only list with no management affordances, not an edit surface that only 403s when they act.

Contract. (e1) is the in-flight GET /apps skeleton (aria-busy). (e2) is a truly empty registry; (e3) is a non-empty registry with a search/filter that matched nothing — distinct states with distinct copy and recovery. (e4) is a non-2xx with data-action="retry". (e5) is the rule: a 403 is an authorization outcome — the list still renders read-only, every management affordance is absent (not disabled-on-click), and it is never a sign-in redirect (only a 401 re-authenticates). This is what stops shipping edit buttons that only fail on save.
