Contrast · a key that does not diverge

The chain must be as readable when nothing is overridden. "No differences" is a real answer to "why is this value what it is" and needs its own designed treatment — not an empty diff table.

No overrides anywhere. notifications.retry.maxAttempts is 5 at every scope, straight from defaultValue. Nothing in the chain has changed it.

Contrast · a chain terminated by a lock

A lock does not merely win — it stops resolution and refuses writes beneath. The chain view must show the scopes below the lock as unreachable, not merely as "no override", or an admin will keep trying to set them.

false 🔒 ← portal Acme Portal — locked, in force lockReason: "SMS is disabled tenant-wide pending carrier review."
unreachable × org Acme Corp Writes refused with LOCKED_BY_ANCESTOR while the portal lock stands.
unreachable × user YouSame — beneath the lock.

Contract. The definition panel is GET /v1/namespaces/{namespace}/keys/{key} → KeyDefinition. The chain is built from GET /v1/config read at each scope in the chain — every Scope carries its scopeType, so no step is identified by a bare id.
precedence is not cosmetic. Under least-specific-wins the same chain resolves the other way and the arrows reverse — the view reads definition.precedence rather than assuming most-specific.
