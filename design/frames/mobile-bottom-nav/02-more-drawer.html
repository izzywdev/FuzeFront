Coexistence contract. The More tab and the top-bar hamburger both call the shell's single toggleSidebar (Layout.tsx isSidebarOpen) — one drawer, two triggers, no second overlay primitive. While open: the drawer is role="dialog" aria-modal="true", the scrim (--scrim) covers content and bar, the backgrounded content is aria-hidden, More shows data-pressed + aria-expanded="true", and the drawer honours --safe-area-top/-bottom so a full-height panel clears the notch and home indicator. The drawer holds everything the four bar slots can't — Profile, Billing, Connectors, Help, Status, plus the registry Apps section and (role-gated) Admin — reusing SidePanel's existing markup. Dismiss: scrim tap (data-action='close-drawer'), ✕, Esc, or any item.
