Contract. The bar is position: fixed at the viewport bottom at --bottom-nav-height (56px) + --safe-area-bottom; the content area's padding-bottom clears it so nothing is hidden under the bar or the home indicator. Exactly one tab carries aria-current="page", derived from the router pathname (a /app/<slug> route lights Apps). Each tab is a real <a>/<button> at ≥--touch-target (44px) tall. The More tab is a <button> with aria-haspopup/aria-expanded that opens the drawer (Frame 02) — it never navigates. The badge is an accessible count, not decoration.
Test hooks: [data-frame='01-bottom-nav'] · [data-component='bottom-nav'] · [data-nav-item='home'|'apps'|'orgs'|'more'] · [aria-current='page'] · [data-action='toggle-drawer'] · [data-badge]. Tokens only.
