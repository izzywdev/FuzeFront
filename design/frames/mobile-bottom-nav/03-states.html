What QA writes RED specs against

  • Loading never blanks the bar. [data-component='bottom-nav'][data-state='loaded'] is present from first paint; only [data-panel='drawer'][data-state='loading'] shows skeletons (aria-busy). The bar is not gated on the registry fetch.
  • Empty keeps navigation. [data-empty] lives in the content, the bar still renders all four tabs; the empty state offers [data-action='browse-apps'], not a dead end.
  • Error is fail-open and scoped. [data-error] is confined to the drawer's Apps section with [data-action='retry']; Home / Orgs / More and the Portal section stay usable.
  • Safe-area. [data-safe-area='true'] — the bar's height is calc(--bottom-nav-height + --safe-area-bottom) and content padding-bottom clears it; the top bar pads --safe-area-top.
  • Touch targets. every .bn-item is ≥--touch-target (44px, WCAG 2.5.5).
  • Reduced motion. drawer/scrim transitions collapse to instant under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Long labels truncate (text-overflow: ellipsis) — never wrap or overflow the slot.
