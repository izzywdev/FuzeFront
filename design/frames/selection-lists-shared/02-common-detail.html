A common list, shared by every organization

FuzeFront provides this list to every organization. You can view it and pick its values in any form, but you can't change it here — a change would affect every organization at once.

To change it for Globex Freight, fork it: your organization gets its own copy with the same key (priority). If you share the fork with everyone in Globex Freight (the default), every form in your organization that uses priority switches to it; a private draft is used only by people you give a role, and everyone else keeps the common list. Values already saved in records keep showing correctly.

Why the Fork CTA is shown before anyone tries to edit

The UI cannot know in advance whether you may create lists — there is no capability probe for SelectionListCatalog:create, and a viewer does not hold it. So the CTA renders whenever visibility = platform, editable = false and the effective list for this key is this list. The service decides: a viewer who presses it gets the 403 explanation in the dialog (fork results), and the CTA is then replaced by that explanation for the rest of the session. The CTA is not rendered when editable is absent (a 4.0.0 service), when the list is not platform, or when your organization already has its own version of the key (see states).
