Rules the implementation must keep

  • One lookup, one list, at every width — the effective list for the key is decided by the service; the phone layout never fetches or shows a second list.
  • Layout is the approved 375 px picker (selection-lists frame 4d): full field width, menu inline under the control, options ≥ 44 px.
  • The opt-in source caption wraps under the control and never widens the field; it is absent unless showSource is set.
  • A redirected stored value shows no visible change at phone width either — no badge, notice or flicker; the value submitted is unchanged unless the user picks.
  • RTL: the host's dir drives the picker; keys and organization names are bidi-isolated; the common/fork glyphs are not mirrored.
