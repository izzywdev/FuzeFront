Rules the implementation must keep

  • One component, two presentations: ForkListDialog renders the DS Modal at ≥ 40em and the DS BottomSheet below it. Content, order, hooks and focus rules are identical; only the container changes.
  • The sheet's facts are condensed to Key and Copied; “You become owner” and the quota preview move below the options and may scroll. The visibility choice, the no-sync note and both actions are always visible without scrolling at 375×667.
  • Actions stack vertically, full width: Cancel first, the primary action bottom-most (nearest the thumb). In RTL the order is the same — stacking is vertical, so nothing is mirrored.
  • Organization names and keys inside Hebrew/Arabic sentences are wrapped in <bdi> / dir="ltr"; quotation marks follow the locale („…” in Hebrew, «…» in Arabic).
  • Swipe-down and the scrim dismiss the sheet only while idle — never during submitting (frame 5).
