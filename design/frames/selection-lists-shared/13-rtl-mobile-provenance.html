Rules the implementation must keep

  • At ≤ 40em the provenance card stacks: glyph and title, then the facts as a two-column definition list, then “View common list” as a full-width button. Badges wrap; nothing scrolls sideways at 375 px.
  • The header action is the same gate as desktop, rendered as a full-width button under the card: “Revert to common list…” only after the source read returned an active list, “Archive fork…” when it is archived or gone, nothing while checking or failed.
  • Both confirmations are BottomSheets (role="alertdialog") with the same consequence text as frame 3a, actions stacked full-width with the destructive one last. The safe action holds initial focus; swipe-down dismisses only while idle.
  • RTL: dates, revision numbers, keys and list names are bidi-isolated; quotation marks follow the locale. The fork glyph (⑂) is not mirrored, the row chevron is.
  • Touch targets are at least the DS --touch-target (44 px).
