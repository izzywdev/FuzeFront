Where phone verification sits in signup

The phone step runs immediately after the account is created, in parallel with email verification — not after it. Signup (POST /v1/security/signup → 201 LoginResponse) already establishes a session, and every phone-verify endpoint is a session-bound call, so the step can run the moment signup returns. The email link is already on its way while the user enters their phone code.

Why here, and why in parallel

  • The session exists already. /verify/phone/start and /verify/status call requireBearer in backend/security/src/routes/security.ts. Signup returns a session, so the step needs no extra sign-in. If it ran after email verification, a user who opens the email link on another device would land there with no session and would have to sign in again.
  • Email verification doesn't block, so the phone step shouldn't wait for it. The approved check-inbox screen already says "you can keep working". If the phone step waited behind the email link, a slow mail service would hold up SMS too, even though SMS doesn't depend on email at all.
  • Phone first, inbox screen second. The user has their phone in hand and the code arrives in seconds. By the time they finish, the email link is usually waiting. If the order were reversed, the user would sit on the "check your inbox" screen first and then have to come back for the phone step.
  • Approved flows stay unchanged. 01-signup's submit now routes to /signup/phone instead of /signup/verify when the step applies. No approved frame is edited. The route change is a delta noted in the manifest, and it only happens behind the flag.
