Data. AppPublication: tier (Visibility) drives the tier badge; status ∈ none | pending | approved | rejected drives the panel; rejection.reason is shown as text (escaped, never HTML). Names for requestedBy come from the creator/member lookup where available; otherwise the date alone is shown.
Visibility of the panel. Rendered only when the flag is ON and GET /apps/{slug}/publication returns 200 — the contract answers 404 to anyone who can merely read the app, so a 404 means “no panel”, not an error. A 409 on submit is a race, not a failure: refetch and render (f3)/(f4).
