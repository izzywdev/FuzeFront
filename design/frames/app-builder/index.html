App Builder & Ownership · Approval Frames

Three capabilities from docs/planning/app-builder-and-ownership.md, in four flows: a Build your application card that starts a FuzeAgent session which develops and deploys an app into your personal workspace or the active org; an org-held, user-originated ownership block (the org owns the app, its creator is the contact while a member); and the marketplace publish request with its platform-admin review queue. All four ship dark behind default-OFF release flags. Approve per flow.

Flow 1 · Build entry · route /applications · BuildEntryFlow · fuzefront.apps.build-with-agent
Frame (a) · /applications

“Build your application” card

Sibling of the dashed Add card. Flag OFF ⇒ not rendered; flag resolving ⇒ treated as OFF; in-flight session ⇒ resume shortcut.
Flow 2 · Build session · route /applications/build[/:sessionId] · BuildSessionFlow · fuzefront.apps.build-with-agent
Frame (b) · /applications/build

Describe the app

Where (personal / active org), name, brief ≤ 4000. Validation and submitting states.
Frame (c) · /applications/build/:sessionId

Session in progress

launching → building → deploying → deployed, FuzeAgent deep link (same-origin only), cancel confirm, loading.
Frame (d) · /applications/build[/:sessionId]

Outcomes, failures & refusals

Deployed (tile appears), failed, cancelled, 502 launch failed, 503 builder_unavailable / feature_disabled, 403, 409, 429, 404.
Flow 3 · Creator ownership · app detail · AppOwnershipCard · fuzefront.apps.creator-ownership
Frame (e) · /apps/:appId

Owner & creator

Org as owner; creator name + contact while a member, “former member” with no contact after; personal; not recorded; flag OFF.
Flow 4 · Marketplace publish · app detail + /admin/marketplace-requests · MarketplacePublishFlow · fuzefront.apps.marketplace-publishing
Frame (f) · /apps/:appId

Publish to marketplace

Request with notes; pending; approved; rejected with reason + resubmit; 409 already pending / published; read-only.
Frame (g) · /admin/marketplace-requests

Admin review queue

Platform admins: approve, or reject with a required reason; 409 NOT_PENDING race; empty, loading, error, 403.
