Contract gap — commissioned by approval. app-registry 1.2.0 has approve/reject per app but no way to list pending requests. This queue needs an additive, platform-admin-only, cursor-paginated GET /publication-requests?status=pending returning AppPublication plus the app's display name, icon and organizationId (flag-gated like its siblings). contract-designer owns that amendment (1.3.0, minor); until it lands this flow cannot be implemented — the frames do not invent a client-side scan of GET /apps.
Decisions. Approve → visibility=marketplace; Reject → { reason }, the app may be resubmitted. Both remove the row optimistically and restore it on any non-2xx except 409 NOT_PENDING (g4). Publisher is the owning org; the contact column follows the frame (e) rule — a former member is never named. Nav entry lives beside the other admin consoles and renders only for platform admins.
