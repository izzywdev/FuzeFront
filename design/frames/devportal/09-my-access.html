My access — membership, quota, sandbox keys

What the signed-in developer actually holds, stated plainly: their root-org Developer membership, what that role does and does not grant, their sandbox quota, and their sandbox keys. Every state a real account can be in is below — including the two failure states people forget: provisioning still in flight on very first sign-in, and access denied.

States

(a) No keys yet
🔑

No sandbox keys

You don't need a key to use the Playground in the browser — only to call the sandbox from a terminal or CI.

(b) Provisioning · very first sign-in, membership grant still in flight
⏳

Setting up your developer access…

Your developer membership on the platform root organization is being created and synced to the policy engine. This usually takes a few seconds. The Catalog and Playground stay disabled until it completes — we don't show them as working and then 403 on the first click.

(c) Denied · 403 — provisioning failed, or the capability is flag-gated off
🔒

Developer access isn't enabled for your account

You are signed in as dana.okafor@fuzefront.com, but no developer membership exists on the platform root organization. Either automatic provisioning did not complete, or fuzefront.devportal.enabled is currently limited to a smaller audience. Nothing is partially enabled — the whole portal fails closed rather than exposing a subset.

(d) Error · access/quota lookup failed
⚠

Couldn't load your access

GET /api/v1/devportal/me/access failed. We can't tell you what you hold right now, so we don't guess — nothing on this page is rendered from a default or a cached assumption.

(e) Revoke confirmation · destructive, so it is explicit
⚠

Revoke sandbox key “ci-smoke”?

Anything using this key stops working immediately. This cannot be undone; create a new key instead of restoring this one.
