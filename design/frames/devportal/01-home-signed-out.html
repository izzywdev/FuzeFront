🛡

The Playground never touches production.

Every "Try it" call is brokered by devportal-service to a spec-generated mock server, or to a sandbox URL a repo has explicitly opted into. Your real session token is never forwarded — the proxy mints a synthetic sandbox identity scoped only to that target.
