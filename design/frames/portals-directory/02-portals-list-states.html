States

States are contract, not decoration. The directory ships with every one of these or it ships a happy-path-only launcher. Loading, the fresh-install empty case, a failed list with retry, and — the load-bearing one — the fail-closed permission-denied / read-only case: a caller without manage authority sees no launch affordances at all. A 403 is an authorization result shown in place, never a sign-in redirect (only a 401 re-authenticates).
