# Navigating the Portal

The FuzeFront portal is a shell: a fixed frame (top bar and left sidebar) around whichever page or app you are using. This article describes each part of that frame and what you can do from it.

## The top bar

From left to right, the top bar contains:

- **Menu button** (three horizontal bars). Shown only on narrow screens such as phones; it opens the sidebar as a slide-in drawer.
- **Brand mark.** The FuzeFront logo, or the organization's own branding if your portal is white-labeled.
- **Organization selector.** Shows the name of your active context: an organization name, or **Personal** where that context is enabled. Select it to switch.
- **App launcher** (grid icon). A compact grid of the apps you can open.
- **Theme toggle.** Switches between the light and dark themes.
- **Notifications bell and avatar menu.** The bell opens your notification inbox; the avatar opens the account menu described below.

## The left sidebar

The sidebar has these sections, top to bottom:

1. **Apps.** The apps activated for you, each with its icon and menu label. Selecting one opens it in the portal.
2. **Portal.** Platform pages: **Applications**, **Organizations**, **Profile**, **Billing**, and **Connectors**. Additional entries such as **Portals**, **Selection Lists**, **Configuration**, or **Staff console** appear only when they are enabled for your deployment and your account is allowed to use them. **Admin Panel** appears for platform administrators.
3. **App menu.** While an app is open, items the app contributes appear here.
4. **Help** and **Status** at the bottom, always reachable. Help opens this Help Center; Status opens the system status page (see [Getting help](getting-help.md)).

The sidebar scrolls if it is taller than the window; Help and Status stay pinned at the bottom.

### When an app takes over the menu

Some apps replace the portal menu with their own. When that happens the sidebar shows the app's name and its own items, plus a **Return to portal** control at the top. That control is always supplied by the portal, never by the app, so you can always get back to the Dashboard.

## The app launcher

The grid icon in the top bar lists activated apps as icon-and-name tiles. Select a tile to open the app. Apps that require an organization are hidden while you are in the **Personal** context; switch to an organization to see them.

## The avatar menu

Select your avatar to open a menu with several sections:

- **Accounts.** You can stay signed in to several accounts in the same browser (up to five) and switch between them. **Add account** signs in another one; **Sign out** on a row signs out of just that account.
- **Context or Organization.** Choose one of your organizations, or **Personal** where the personal context is enabled for your deployment. If you have none, you can **Create organization** from here.
- **Language.** Choose the portal language. See below.
- **Profile**, **Settings** (opens [Account and security](account-and-security.md)), and **Sign out**. Platform administrators also see **Admin Panel**.

## Language and right-to-left layout

The portal is available in English and Hebrew. Choosing Hebrew switches the whole layout to right-to-left: the sidebar, menus, and text direction all mirror. Your choice is remembered in the browser you made it in, so set it again on a new device or browser. The language list may show additional languages; where the portal has no translation for a string it falls back to English.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar opens an inbox. When there is nothing new it shows an empty state, and if the inbox cannot load it shows an error you can retry.

## Switching context changes what you see

Your active context (Personal or an organization) filters the Dashboard, launcher, and sidebar, and decides whose billing you are viewing. If an app you expect is missing, check the context first. See [Organizations and teams](organizations-and-teams.md) and [Installing and using apps](installing-and-using-apps.md).

For a first-time walkthrough, see [Getting started](getting-started.md). On a phone, see [Mobile app](mobile-app.md).
